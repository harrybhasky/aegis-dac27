TITLE
Aegis: Clock-Domain-Aware Guardrails for Agentic LLM Timing Optimization of Multi-Clock RTL

ABSTRACT (~95 words; no author names, as required for double-blind review)
LLM agents now improve RTL timing by iterating rewrite, synthesis, and equivalence checking. On multi-clock designs this gate is unsound: equivalence checking and simulation cannot see clock-domain-crossing (CDC) hazards. We observe a reproduced state-of-the-art agent promoting a formally equivalent rewrite that duplicates a synchronizer stage. We present Aegis, which classifies every crossing on the elaborated netlist, locks logic its verifiers cannot check, optimizes per clock domain, and re-checks crossings and CDC contracts on every candidate. On open-source multi-clock designs, Aegis never promotes a CDC-unsafe design, with competitive timing and lower LLM cost.
